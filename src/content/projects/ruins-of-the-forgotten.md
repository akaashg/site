Ruins of the Forgotten is a first-person survival horror game about a forest ranger stranded in a jungle altered by a supernatural anomaly. Players manage hunger, thirst, and health while the wildlife turns hostile after dark.

## What I am building

My work spans custom HLSL shaders for the jungle's day and night atmosphere, animation systems for peaceful-to-hostile creature transformations, and AI behaviour trees that respond to time of day and player state. The islands are procedurally generated, so no two playthroughs share the same layout.

## Where it stands

The public Steam demo is live, with Early Access planned for Q3 2026 and an expected 4-6 month iteration period. The current build brings together 3 production systems - procedural islands, day/night-driven wildlife AI, and custom atmosphere/animation work - into one playable survival loop.
