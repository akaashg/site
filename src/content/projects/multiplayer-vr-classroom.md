## The problem (draft)

Structured training — especially medical, industrial, or clinical procedures — needs an instructor guiding multiple trainees through a fixed sequence of steps inside a shared 3D space, not a free-roam simulation where nobody follows the same script.

## What I built (draft)

Engineered 3D simulation modules with realistic physics interactions and step-based guided instruction workflows on Photon Fusion, multiplayer, on Meta Quest — built with an eye toward medical, industrial, and clinical training applications.
